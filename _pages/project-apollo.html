Context

This work concerns delegated recovery and secure encrypted vaults, in connection with my work at EPFL DEDIS.

The related Apollo paper studies social vault recovery: how trusted contacts can help restore access to encrypted data, while protecting recovery metadata and reducing what a user needs to remember. Read the paper on arXiv.
