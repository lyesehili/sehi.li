Research & work experience

Feb. 2026 – Present

Research Assistant · EPFL DEDIS

Decentralized and Distributed Systems Laboratory, Lausanne

For my bachelor’s thesis, completed in June 2026:

  • Designed and implemented delegated recovery for Apollo in Go using Schnorr signatures over Edwards25519. Evaluated the implementation across 19 correctness scenarios.
  • Modelled quorum completion with a binomial model and 10,000 Monte Carlo trials per configuration. Estimated 99.63% quorum completion with five delegates and a threshold of three, given an available initiating delegate and a 90% independent response probability for each remaining delegate.
  • Benchmarked verification latency and estimated communication overhead. Identified delegate discovery as the main operational bottleneck and ran a ten-person pilot on the mobile and web prototypes.

I’m now working on Apollo Usability Research. The study aims to recruit more than 200 participants to compare Apollo configurations and assess how people complete tasks and understand social recovery.
