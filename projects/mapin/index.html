Map'In

An Android app for discovering student events and sharing location-based memories, developed by a team of seven at EPFL.

Status
Completed
Technologies
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase, Mapbox GL, OpenRouter

What we built

Students can create or join events on the map and share photos and videos afterward, tagging friends who were there.

The app also supports friend lists, chat, directions, saved map regions for offline use, and AI-powered event recommendations.

Design & implementation

The Android app separates Jetpack Compose screens, domain logic, and data access. It uses MVVM with repositories, Firebase for authentication, storage, data, and messaging, Mapbox for maps, and OpenRouter for the recommendations assistant.
