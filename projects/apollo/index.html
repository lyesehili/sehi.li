Apollo Usability Research

Investigating the usability of social recovery and how people understand the security mechanisms behind Apollo.

Status
In progress
Platforms
Mobile and web

Apollo lets people recover access to encrypted vaults with help from trusted contacts.

From the pilot to a larger study

In the ten-person pilot for my bachelor’s thesis, participants could often complete a recovery task while still being unsure how backup distribution worked, what the recovery threshold meant, or what their contacts were supposed to do. This raises a question for the larger study: what does completing the task tell us about how well people understand the underlying security model?

I’m working on a study that aims to recruit more than 200 participants. We plan to compare Apollo configurations and examine usability, task completion, and how well participants understand social recovery.

Publication plans

Once the study is complete, we plan to incorporate its findings into a revision of the existing Apollo paper and submit the revision for publication.

Red Cross and RedSafe

We’re also in the early stages of exploring possible work involving the Red Cross and RedSafe.
